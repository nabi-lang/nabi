The Rust standard library (core, alloc, std, panic_abort, unwind) of Rust
1.85.1, linked into bin/nabi, bin/nabic and bin/nabils, and core with
compiler_builtins into lib/libnabir.a (and the libnabir.a of each target
under lib/nabi/targets/), so into every program nabic links. The libc
crate 0.2.191 is linked into the same binaries and runtimes.
